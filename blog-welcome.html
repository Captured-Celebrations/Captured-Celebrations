Welcome to Captured Celebrations

JM Captures came to be little by little.

I always knew that photography was something that helped calm my mind. I enjoyed taking nature photos, but I never thought it would turn into anything more. At the time, it was simply something that helped me through difficult moments — a way to quiet my mind when I needed it most.

Over time, photography became more than just a hobby. It became something I truly connected with.

It wasn’t until a friend suggested that I start reaching out to others around the Chatham-Kent area that I began to take things more seriously. I started offering free mini sessions and slowly began building a client base.

Over the years, those sessions turned into returning clients — and clients who trust me to capture different styles and moments for them. That trust has given me the opportunity to grow, experiment, and truly start finding my niche.

Captured Celebrations is more than just photography.

It’s about capturing the moment — and creating it.

This is where photography and event planning come together. Not only capturing memories, but helping create meaningful experiences that you’ll never forget.

  • And occasional exclusive offers
