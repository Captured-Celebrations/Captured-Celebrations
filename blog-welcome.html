Welcome to Captured Celebrations

JM Captures came to be little by little.

I always knew that photography was something that helped calm my mind. I enjoyed taking nature photos, but I never thought it would turn into anything more. At the time, it was simply something that helped me through difficult moments — a way to quiet my mind when I needed it most. Over time, photography became more than just a hobby for me — it became something I truly connected with.

It wasn’t until a friend suggested that I start reaching out to others around the Chatham-Kent area that I began to take things more seriously. I started offering free mini sessions and slowly began building a client base.

Over the years, those sessions turned into returning clients — and clients who trust me to capture different styles and moments for them. That trust has given me the opportunity to grow, experiment, and truly start finding my niche.

While creating and booking sessions, I began to notice something more. I wasn’t just taking photos — I was planning them. I was styling them. I was thinking about the little details that helped bring everything together.

That naturally carried into events as well. I found myself helping plan and style different parts of events for clients, and that’s when the idea came to me to grow JM Captures into Captured Celebrations.

Captured Celebrations is more than just photography. It’s about capturing the moment — and creating it.

Whether it’s a birthday, a wedding anniversary, an engagement, a bridal or baby shower, a gender reveal, or any other meaningful event, I love being part of it all.

I love watching an idea go from something small into something real. Seeing the planning process come together, watching the styling bring the vision to life, and most importantly, watching clients realize that what they imagined is no longer just in their minds — it’s real, and it’s actually happening.

When clients book with Captured Celebrations, whether it’s for an event or a photography session, they can expect respect, dignity, excitement, and positivity. I also understand that not every event carries the same emotions — whether it’s a memorial or a celebration of life, I approach every moment with the care and understanding it deserves.

Looking ahead, there are some exciting sessions coming up.

Mother’s Day Mini Sessions will be taking place at Two Creeks Conservation Area in Wheatley, Ontario. I’m really looking forward to these sessions and capturing the bonds between mothers and their children — and even some generational moments.

After that, Spring Mini Sessions for families will be held at Wilson’s Conservation Area. It will be a nice change of scenery, and I’m excited to continue creating meaningful moments in a new setting.

This blog will be a space where I share behind-the-scenes moments, session highlights, planning inspiration, and exclusive offers. From time to time, blog readers will get early access to upcoming sessions and special promotions — so it’s definitely something to keep an eye on.

If you’re looking to book a session or start planning your next event, I would love to be part of it. You can book your session through the website or reach out to start bringing your ideas to life.

Thank you for being here and for supporting Captured Celebrations. I’m so excited for everything that’s ahead.
