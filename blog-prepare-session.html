How to Prepare for Your Photo Session

Preparing for your photo session doesn’t have to feel stressful or overwhelming.

With a little bit of planning — and the right mindset — it can actually be something you look forward to and enjoy from start to finish.

What Should You Wear?

When it comes to outfits, I always want my clients to feel comfortable first. You’re welcome to choose what feels best for you, and I do offer guidance if needed.

Small logos are okay. Larger logos or bold graphics are best avoided, as they can be distracting in photos.

For couples and group sessions, coordinating outfits rather than perfectly matching tends to photograph really nicely. That said, I completely understand that not every session will be perfectly coordinated — and that’s okay.

Preparing for the Day

If you’re able to, take things slow — enjoy your morning, have your coffee, and give yourself space to relax before your session. Even arriving a few minutes early can make a big difference.

Tips for Families — Especially with Kids

When it comes to working with children, I like to meet them where they’re at. If they love jokes, I’m ready with jokes. If they’re into cool science facts, I’ll do my homework too.

Please don’t stress if your child isn’t fully cooperating. That’s completely normal. Sometimes all it takes is a little bit of time to warm up.

It’s Okay to Feel Nervous

A lot of clients worry about how they’ll look, how to pose, or whether they’ll feel awkward. But once we start, that feeling usually fades pretty quickly.

You’ll probably hear me say: “Just be yourself, feel what you feel, and go with the flow.”

Timing & Lighting

The best time for photos is typically early in the morning or later in the evening as the sun begins to set. As for weather — I can work in almost anything, as long as nothing is falling from the sky.
