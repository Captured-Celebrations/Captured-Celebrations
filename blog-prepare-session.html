How to Prepare for Your Photo Session

Preparing for your photo session doesn’t have to feel stressful or overwhelming.

With a little bit of planning — and the right mindset — it can actually be something you look forward to and enjoy from start to finish.

What Should You Wear?

When it comes to outfits, I always want my clients to feel comfortable first.

You’re welcome to choose what feels best for you, and I do offer guidance if needed. In your session questionnaire, I’ll ask if you’d like help with outfit choices — and I’m always happy to give suggestions.

As a general guideline:

  • Small logos are okay
  • Larger logos or bold graphics are best avoided, as they can be distracting in photos

For couples and group sessions, coordinating outfits rather than perfectly matching tends to photograph really nicely. That said, I completely understand that not every session will be perfectly coordinated — and that’s okay.

At the end of the day, my goal is to create images that you love. While I can guide and edit, what matters most is that you feel good in what you’re wearing.

Preparing for the Day

The way you prepare for your session can really shape your overall experience.

I always take time throughout my day to prepare calmly, and I encourage clients to do the same when possible. If you’re able to, take things slow — enjoy your morning, have your coffee, and give yourself space to relax before your session.

I understand that not everyone can take the day off, but even arriving a few minutes early can make a big difference. It gives you time to settle into the environment, look around, and feel comfortable before we begin.

Tips for Families — Especially with Kids

When it comes to working with children, I like to meet them where they’re at.

If they love jokes — I’m ready with jokes. If they’re into cool science facts — I’ll do my homework too. If they’re part of the 6–7 age crew — I’m right there with them.

I also like to work with parents. You know your child best, so I’ll often ask for your input on what works for them.

Please don’t stress if your child isn’t fully cooperating. That’s completely normal.

Sometimes all it takes is a little bit of time to warm up. If needed, I’m more than happy to meet with you and your family before the session so that when the time comes, everyone feels comfortable and familiar.

And yes — if snacks help keep things positive, bring the snacks. I’m all for anything that keeps smiles on faces.

It’s Okay to Feel Nervous

It’s completely normal to feel both excited and nervous before your session.

A lot of clients worry about how they’ll look, how to pose, or whether they’ll feel awkward. But once we start, that feeling usually fades pretty quickly.

You’ll probably hear me say: “Just be yourself, feel what you feel, and go with the flow.”

One Thing I Wish Every Client Knew

It’s okay to feel unprepared.

You don’t have to have everything figured out before your session — that’s what I’m here for.

I’ll guide you, support you, and make sure you feel comfortable and confident every step of the way.

Timing & Lighting

The best time for photos is typically early in the morning or later in the evening as the sun begins to set.

Even after dinner, when the sun isn’t at its highest, can be a great time.

As for weather — I can work in almost anything, as long as nothing is falling from the sky. Cold or warm, as long as the skies are clear, we’re good to go.
