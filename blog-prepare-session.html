How to Prepare for Your Photo Session

Preparing for your session doesn’t have to feel stressful — it should feel exciting.

Wear what makes you feel comfortable. Avoid large logos, and try to coordinate outfits when possible.

Arriving a little early helps you settle in and feel more relaxed.

For kids — snacks are ALWAYS a good idea. And don’t worry if they need time to warm up — that’s completely normal.

It’s okay to feel nervous at first. Once we start, that usually fades quickly.

Just be yourself, go with the flow, and enjoy the moment.
