What to Expect From a Mini Session

Mini sessions are typically 20 minutes long and are designed to capture a specific moment in time.

Unlike full or basic sessions, which tell more of your overall story, mini sessions focus on a single chapter — whether that’s Mother’s Day, Back to School, Christmas, Valentine’s Day, Easter, Spring, or another meaningful moment. They’re a way to pause and capture how things feel right now.

A Relaxed Experience — Even in a Short Time

Even though mini sessions are shorter, I always aim to keep the experience relaxed and natural.

With younger children or larger families, things can feel a little fast-paced at times — but that doesn’t mean it has to feel stressful. My goal is to keep everything flowing smoothly so you can actually enjoy the moment.

No need to worry about getting everything perfect — that’s my job. You just focus on being present and enjoying your time together.

If you have specific poses in mind but aren’t sure how to get into them, I’ll guide you. And if you already know what you’re doing — even better.

Before your session, I also ask whether you prefer posed photos, candid moments, or a mix of both. I use your answers to shape your session, which means no two sessions are ever exactly the same.

What Happens During Your Session

When you arrive, I’ll welcome you into the space, introduce myself, and help you settle into the vibe of the session.

Family sessions are one of my favourites — I love seeing the connections, laughter, and genuine energy that comes through. Those little in-between moments are often the most meaningful.

To make the most of our 20 minutes, I typically break the session into 3–4 parts. I come prepared with poses and prompts, especially for more candid moments, so we can move smoothly from one part to the next while capturing a variety of images you’ll love.

What You’ll Receive

Mini session clients receive:

  • The best 10 professionally edited images
  • A digital gallery
  • Gallery delivery typically within 2 weeks of the session date

Because of the time structure of mini sessions, additional photos are not available for purchase. This helps keep things fair for all clients.

If you’re looking for more images or a longer, more flexible experience, a Basic Session may be a better fit for you.

Why I Love Mini Sessions

Mini sessions are one of my favourite things to offer.

I get to see so many familiar faces — and hopefully meet some new ones too — all in one day. It’s a full day of connection, laughter, and meaningful moments, and that kind of energy is something I truly love being part of.

A Note for Anyone Feeling Nervous

If you’re ever unsure or a little nervous about your session, you’re not alone.

You can always share any concerns in your questionnaire, and I’ll make sure to guide you every step of the way. My goal is for you to feel relaxed, comfortable, and confident — so that your photos reflect real, genuine moments.
