What to Expect

From the moment you book, your experience is designed to feel easy, comfortable, and tailored to you.

Before Your Session

We’ll talk through your vision, location ideas, outfit styles, and preferences — whether you love posed photos, candid moments, or a mix of both. This helps create a session that feels natural, comfortable, and true to you.

During Your Session

Sessions are relaxed and easygoing. I’ll guide you when needed while also allowing natural movement and genuine moments to unfold, so nothing feels forced or uncomfortable.

After Your Session

Your images are carefully edited to reflect the Captured Celebrations style. Galleries are typically delivered within one week, or up to two weeks during busier times.

Each session includes a set number of images, with the option to purchase additional downloads once your gallery is delivered.

Your Photos

If you’d like small editing adjustments — such as converting an image to black and white — requests can be made within 14 days of your gallery delivery.

Galleries remain active for 30 days, so be sure to download and save all the images you love within that time.

Why Choose Captured Celebrations

Captured Celebrations began in 2024 under the name JM Captures, starting with simple photography sessions that focused on preserving meaningful moments in a natural way.

Over time, that passion grew into something more. Alongside photography, event planning and styling became part of the vision — creating a brand that offers not only beautiful images, but also thoughtful support behind the moments being celebrated.

What makes these sessions different is the level of care and intention behind each one. From discussing outfits and preferred tones, to choosing the right time of day and understanding the story you want to tell, every detail is considered with purpose.

Above all, every client is treated with dignity, respect, and understanding. Comfort is at the center of everything I do. While I may be the one behind the camera, you are the one in the photo — and your comfort, confidence, and experience always come first.

Clients can expect an easy-going, relaxed experience that allows genuine moments to unfold naturally while still capturing the images they truly want.
