What to Expect

From the moment you book, everything is designed to feel easy, comfortable, and tailored to you.

Your Session, Your Style

Every client is different, which is why I ask about your preferences ahead of time. Whether you love posed photos, candid moments, or a mixture of both, your session will reflect what feels most natural to you.

Comfort Comes First

I want every client to feel comfortable, confident, relaxed, and excited. I’ll guide you when needed while also leaving room for genuine, raw emotion to unfold naturally.

Editing & Delivery

Your images are carefully edited to match the Captured Celebrations style. Turnaround time is typically one week, or up to two weeks during busier times such as mini sessions or larger bookings.

Photo Options

The number of images you receive depends on the package selected at booking. Basic, Full, and Event sessions offer additional photo purchases. Mini sessions include a set number of images due to the shorter session time.

Why Choose Captured Celebrations

Captured Celebrations began in 2024 under the name JM Captures, starting with simple photography sessions that focused on preserving meaningful moments in a natural way.

Over time, that passion grew into something more. Alongside photography, event planning and styling became part of the vision — creating a brand that offers not only beautiful images, but also thoughtful support behind the moments being celebrated.

What makes these sessions different is the level of care and intention behind each one. From discussing outfits and preferred tones, to choosing the right time of day and understanding the story you want to tell, every detail is considered with purpose.

Above all, every client is treated with dignity, respect, and understanding. Comfort is at the center of everything I do. While I may be the one behind the camera, you are the one in the photo — and your comfort, confidence, and experience always come first.

Clients can expect an easy-going, relaxed experience that allows genuine moments to unfold naturally while still capturing the images they truly want.
