What to Expect From a Mini Session

Mini sessions are typically 20 minutes long and are designed to capture a specific moment in time.

Unlike full sessions, they focus on a single chapter — like Mother's Day, Christmas, or Spring sessions.

Even though they're short, I keep everything relaxed and natural. You don’t need to stress — that’s my job!

I guide when needed, but I also let real moments happen naturally.

You’ll receive your best 10 edited photos in a digital gallery, typically within 2 weeks.
